# Ramses keeper operations and alerts

Monitor JSON logs independently of the keeper process. An absent heartbeat cannot report its own failure. Alert when heartbeat age exceeds two loop intervals plus 60 seconds (660 seconds at the default). `fatal`, `attention`, unknown reverts, mined reverts, code-pin changes, role escalation, unexpected signer nonces and ambiguous submission require intervention before restarting signing.

| Signal | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `gasBalanceWei`, `gasReady`, `awaiting_gas` | Alert below 0.0003 ETH and estimate runway from measured gas usage × gas price. Fund only the dedicated keeper; no vault assets belong in this wallet. |
| `checkpointAge` | During a fresh feed, investigate age above one hour plus two polling intervals. Gas caps, validator failures and RPC incidents can delay checkpoints. The contract’s one-day bound is a backstop, not the operating target. |
| `priceMoved50Bps` | Confirm a price-triggered successful checkpoint receipt within the next rate-limited pass when the feed and gas cap permit. It is measured from the persisted confirmed price, not the previous poll. |
| `unrecognisedLossUSD18` | Oracle-valued estimated loss before booking. Investigate any sustained growth relative to the $1,000 pair cap and funded reserve. This is an estimate excluding uncollected rewards, not a loss guarantee; `null` on stale-feed waits means unknown, not zero. |
| `rewardAccrualRaw`, token ID and `externalRewards` | Track raw accrued/received USDG, NVDA and RAM, the reward-token set and validator behavior. RAM has zero NAV. Accrual without successful harvesting, token-list changes or `RewardClaimMismatch` is an alert. |
| Local market cash / vault claim / borrows | The 50% cash target is consumed by borrowing/redemption. Inspect both market balances after maintenance and any caught-failure event; a successful outer receipt alone is insufficient. |
| Pool/gauge/feed/token governance | Review changed code/beacons, fee/protocol share, reward validator, token pauses, feed rounds and governance events. Never auto-update the pin file. |

A stale NVDA feed is a known wait, including weekends and market holidays. Borrowing, collateral redemption and liquidation are unavailable then, while repayment remains possible. Unencumbered holders can consume available local cash at the last booked claim. At 80% collateral factor this accepts unavailable liquidation during price moves, potential bad debt, and loss priority for remaining suppliers. The 50-bps trigger cannot observe a missing feed update. The reserve’s $10/event, $25/day, 50%-coverage limits and 0.0002 NVDA initial funding are not insurance against those risks; no USDG funding amount was recorded in the live reference deployment.

For pending transactions, inspect the public hash, nonce, sender, target, canonical receipt and state. A pending hash is never automatically replaced. A missing hash after intent persistence is ambiguous: stop every instance using that address and reconcile RPC history before editing state. Preserve the journal and manifest. Reverted transactions consumed a nonce but did not perform the requested state transition; never label them successful merely because `cast send` exited 0. Follow a manual acknowledged recovery with a fresh harvest/checkpoint sequence.

The first local live-loop rehearsal observed `RewardClaimMismatch` while trying to harvest/burn a just-minted intermediate full-range NFT. An additional real receipt failed after increasing an aged NFT immediately before recenter. The final worker previews recenter after checkpoint and market maintenance, and skips increasing the old NFT when recenter is due, including the initial full-range bootstrap. Subsequent genuine validator failures remain alerts and stop the keeper; no reward-forfeiture bypass is enabled. The final fork rehearsal ages the NFT before exercising rewarded maintenance and later real swaps. This does not establish every upstream validator policy or future governance behavior.

Disable the service before changing credentials, manifest, deployment or hosting. Drain/reconcile pending transactions and preserve the state directory. Grant a replacement keeper and market operator through the timelock, verify it, revoke the old keeper, and ensure the old process cannot sign before enabling the new one. A timelock-only guardian entails the governance delay; the keeper cannot pause, rescue, reconfigure, bypass the feed, or forward RAM.
